# Timeline (Key Events — Early Origins Focus)

## Pre-2020
- ~2014 onward: NIH/NIAID awards EcoHealth Alliance bat-coronavirus grant (R01AI110964); subawards to WIV begin.
- WIV conducts extensive bat SARS-related coronavirus sampling, sequencing, and experimental work (including chimeric constructs).
- BSL-4 facility at WIV becomes operational ~2018.
- Mar 2018: EcoHealth (with WIV & UNC) submits DEFUSE proposal to DARPA (rejected).

## Late 2019 – Early 2020
- Dec 2019: Early COVID-19 cases cluster around Huanan Seafood Wholesale Market in Wuhan.
- **26 Jan 2020 (Fauci diary):** Market was amplifier, not source; virus jumped from animals somewhere.
- **31 Jan 2020 (Fauci diary):** Call from Jeremy Farrar + Kristian Andersen (Eddie Holmes involved). Scientists felt furin cleavage site mutations “could not have occurred naturally”; possibility of deliberate insertion + accidental/deliberate lab release raised. Fauci recommends larger group.
- **1 Feb 2020 (Fauci diary):** Multi-scientist conference call. Majority felt deliberate insertion possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s years of GOF work on spike/ACE2; Fouchier & Drosten argued natural origin. Fauci: “we could not let this go.”
- Early–mid Feb 2020: Daszak organizes *Lancet* letter (published 19 Feb) condemning lab-origin “conspiracy theories” — 18 days after the private majority concern.
- Early Feb – mid Mar 2020: Drafting and publication of “The proximal origin of SARS-CoV-2” (*Nature Medicine*, 17 Mar). Several authors/participants overlap with the 1 Feb call.

## 2020–2021
- Apr 2020: NIH terminates EcoHealth grant amid pandemic and WIV concerns.
- **2021 (Fauci diary):** Private notes show defensiveness about GOF and lab-origin questions; characterizes many inquiries as “conspiracy theory like letters” and a “waste of our time.”
- **11 May 2021:** Fauci Senate testimony — “The NIH has not ever and does not now fund gain-of-function research in the Wuhan Institute of Virology.”

## 2024–2026
- 2024–Jan 2025: HHS suspends then debars EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak.
- ~24–27 Jul 2026: Sen. Rand Paul releases Fauci diary material.
- 29 Jul 2026: Senate hearing; Fauci invokes Fifth Amendment when asked about origins and the diary.
