# NEIDL (National Emerging Infectious Diseases Laboratories)

**Primary Type:** Research laboratory (BSL-4 / NIAID National Biocontainment Laboratory)  
**Operator:** Boston University  
**Location:** Boston, Massachusetts, USA  
**Relevance:** Second of the two NIAID-funded National Biocontainment Laboratories; high-containment research node in the U.S. landscape

---

## Key documented facts
- NIAID National Biocontainment Laboratory with BSL-4 capacity (approved 2017 after extended review and local controversy).
- Part of the small set of U.S. civilian high-containment labs funded to provide national capacity for emerging infectious disease research.
- Tracked as a comparative node alongside WIV, USAMRIID, Galveston, CDC Atlanta, and the NIAID intramural cluster.
