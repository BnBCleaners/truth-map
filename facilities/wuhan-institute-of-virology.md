# Wuhan Institute of Virology (WIV)

**Primary Type:** Laboratory (high-containment / BSL-4)  
**Operator:** Chinese Academy of Sciences  
**Location:** Wuhan, Hubei, China  
**Relevance:** Central facility in the COVID-origins debate; recipient of U.S. subawards via EcoHealth Alliance; site of extensive bat SARS-related coronavirus research

---

## Key documented facts
- Houses a BSL-4 laboratory (operational ~2018) and lower-containment labs used for coronavirus work.
- Shi Zhengli’s group conducted extensive bat SARS-related coronavirus sampling, sequencing, and experimental work, including chimeric constructs.
- Received U.S. funding via EcoHealth Alliance subawards under NIH grant R01AI110964 for bat coronavirus research.
- Named collaborator on the 2018 DEFUSE proposal (EcoHealth lead) that proposed inserting human-specific cleavage sites into bat SARSr-CoVs (DARPA rejected).
- Biosafety concerns and the proximity of the lab to the early outbreak have made WIV the primary laboratory-origin hypothesis node.
- Chinese non-cooperation has limited independent access to early sequences, databases, personnel health records, and lab notebooks.
