# USAMRIID / Fort Detrick

**Primary Type:** Military laboratory (BSL-4)  
**Operator:** U.S. Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases  
**Location:** Fort Detrick, Frederick, Maryland, USA  
**Relevance:** Primary U.S. military high-containment comparative node to WIV; defensive biodefense mission; historical Amerithrax role; 2019 CDC select-agent suspension

---

## Key documented facts
- Houses one of the principal U.S. BSL-4 laboratories for defensive research on high-consequence pathogens.
- Mission is biodefense (medical countermeasures, threat characterization), not offensive biological weapons (U.S. policy and treaty commitments).
- Played a central investigative role after the 2001 anthrax letters (Amerithrax).
- In 2019 the CDC imposed a temporary suspension of select-agent research after inspection findings; research later resumed under corrective actions.
- Tracked here as a comparative high-containment node alongside WIV and the NIAID national labs so users can see the U.S. landscape in parallel with the Chinese facility at the center of the origins debate.
