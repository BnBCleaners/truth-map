# Galveston National Laboratory (GNL / UTMB)

**Primary Type:** Research laboratory (BSL-4 / NIAID National Biocontainment Laboratory)  
**Operator:** University of Texas Medical Branch (UTMB), Galveston  
**Relevance:** One of two NIAID-funded National Biocontainment Laboratories; documented formal collaboration, training, and MoU relationship with the Wuhan Institute of Virology

---

## Key documented facts
- NIAID National Biocontainment Laboratory (NBL) with BSL-4 capacity.
- Documented collaboration and training relationship with WIV, including a memorandum of understanding that contained provisions later described in oversight materials as including a “memory hole” / limited-retention element.
- Tracked because the formal U.S.–WIV laboratory relationship is material to understanding the network around the facility at the center of the origins debate.
