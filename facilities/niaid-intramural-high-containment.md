# NIAID / NIH Intramural High-Containment Cluster

**Primary Type:** Research laboratories (high-containment / BSL-4 capable nodes)  
**Operator:** National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID / NIH)  
**Locations:** IRF-Frederick (Fort Detrick, MD); Rocky Mountain Laboratories (Hamilton, MT); Bethesda campus facilities  
**Relevance:** NIH’s own intramural high-containment capacity; comparative node to WIV and the extramural NBLs

---

## Key documented facts
- **IRF-Frederick** (Integrated Research Facility at Fort Detrick): NIAID high-containment facility for pathogenesis and medical countermeasure studies. Notes in the broader record include a 2025 safety stand-down / transition period.
- **Rocky Mountain Laboratories (RML):** Long-standing NIAID laboratory in Hamilton, Montana, with high-containment capability and a major historical role in infectious-disease research.
- **Bethesda campus:** Additional NIAID/NIH containment capacity.
- Tracked as a cluster so the U.S. intramural landscape is visible alongside WIV, USAMRIID, Galveston, NEIDL, and CDC Atlanta.
