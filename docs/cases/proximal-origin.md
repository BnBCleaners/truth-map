# CASE: The Proximal Origin of SARS-CoV-2 (March 2020)

**Case ID:** EVENT-2020-PROXIMAL-ORIGIN  
**Status:** Closed (paper exists); Contested (on private vs public framing and effect)  
**Completeness:** 70%

---

## 1. Summary
“The proximal origin of SARS-CoV-2” (Andersen, Rambaut, Lipkin, Holmes, Garry) concluded that SARS-CoV-2 is “not a laboratory construct or a purposefully manipulated virus.” It became the most-cited early scientific statement against laboratory origin. Several authors and facilitators were the same scientists who, on the 1 February 2020 call recorded in Fauci’s diary, had treated deliberate insertion as a live possibility.
