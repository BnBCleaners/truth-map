## 1. Summary
On 19 February 2020, *The Lancet* published a short correspondence signed by 27 scientists titled “Statement in support of the scientists, public health professionals, and medical professionals of China combatting COVID-19.” The letter strongly condemned “conspiracy theories suggesting that COVID-19 does not have a natural origin.” It was organized by Peter Daszak of EcoHealth Alliance. Initial declaration of no competing interests. Later *Lancet* addendum after public criticism of Daszak’s undisclosed WIV collaboration.
