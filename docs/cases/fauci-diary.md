## 1. Summary
In late July 2026, Senator Rand Paul released more than 1,100 pages of Dr. Anthony Fauci’s private typewritten daily notes (“Tony’s Diary,” covering late 2019–2022) plus a 465-page “prequel.” The most consequential entries are from 26 January – 1 February 2020. They record that Fauci was told by leading evolutionary biologists that features around the furin cleavage site appeared difficult to explain by natural evolution and that deliberate insertion was considered possible by a majority of the scientists on the subsequent call. Fauci himself noted Shi Zhengli’s long-running gain-of-function work on coronavirus spike proteins. These private notes sit in the same narrow window that produced the Proximal Origin paper and the Lancet letter. Under project rules, Fauci’s own contemporaneous written words are treated as high-value primary evidence of knowledge and state of mind.

## 2. Key Primary Passages (direct from released diary)

**26 January 2020**  
“It now appears using epi data and genomic data that the first infection was in early December and was not connected to the market. … Now we know the market was not the source, it was the amplifier. Having said that, somewhere the virus jumped from animals to humans.”

**31 January 2020**  
“When I got back to my office, got call from Jeremy Farrar who sounded very concerned and conferenced me in with Kristian Anderson… They had also been talking with Eddie Holmes… The people on the phone felt that the mutations around the furine cleavage site of the spike protein could not have occurred naturally since it would require an evolutionary ‘jump that they found nowhere in bat isolates. They bring up the possibility that this could have been deliberately inserted and either accidentally released or deliberately released by a crazy person in the lab, the former being the most likely. … I suggest that we get a larger group of qualified evolutionary biologists/virologists together…”

**1 February 2020**  
Participants included: Francis Collins, Anthony Fauci, Jeremy Farrar, Patrick Vallance, Kristian Andersen, Christian Drosten, Edward Holmes, Andrew Rambaut, Ron Fouchier, Robert Garry, Mike Ferguson, Marion Koopmans.  
“There was not total agreement about the likelihood of deliberate insertion. Ron Fouchier said he was sure that this could occur naturally and we should not waste our time and divert effort to pursue this. … Also, Christian Drosten was with Ron; the rest felt that deliberate insertion was possible and given the fact that Dr. Zheng-Li Shi at the University of Wuhan has been working for years in GOF in coronaviruses to allow adaptation of the spike protein to bind to the human ACE2 receptor, we could not let this go.”

**2021 entries (selected)**  
Fauci records frustration with congressional and media questions linking NIH funding to a possible lab origin, repeatedly asserts that the specific viruses studied under the EcoHealth grant could not have become SARS-CoV-2, and characterizes many of the inquiries as “conspiracy theory like letters” and a “waste of our time.”
