## Summary

In 1963 the CIA ran a documented wiretapping operation code-named **Project Mockingbird** against syndicated columnists Robert S. Allen and Paul Scott. The taps were authorized at high levels (DCI McCone, coordinating with RFK and others) and identified many of their sources.

Separately, the Church Committee (1975–76) and Carl Bernstein’s 1977 *Rolling Stone* investigation established that the CIA cultivated secret relationships with American journalists and used media channels (especially overseas) for propaganda and intelligence during the Cold War. Church Committee: ~50 official secret relationships. Bernstein: much larger network claimed.

The popular label **“Operation Mockingbird”** for a large systematic domestic media-control program comes mainly from secondary sources (notably Deborah Davis) and remains **Contested** in its stronger forms. Claims that the same continuous program still runs major U.S. media today are labeled **Contested** (see Claim C-11).
