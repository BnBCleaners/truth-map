## Summary

On 18 June 2026, DNI Tulsi Gabbard released a multi-part package of previously classified or internal IC documents concerning COVID-19 origins, U.S. funding related to WIV research, and Anthony Fauci’s interactions with the Intelligence Community.

Official framing stated the materials show Fauci worked with politicized IC leadership to suppress lab-leak discussion and related funding facts, and that he misled Congress. Independent review of the full dump (~67 items / ~391 pages across four Parts) finds:

- Internal IC emails, briefing readouts, and process notes (highest novel value)
- Grant materials and scientific papers related to EcoHealth / WIV coronavirus work
- Congressional correspondence and whistleblower-related materials
- Press clips and media monitoring

The release adds primary internal process documentation. It does **not** contain an intelligence assessment concluding that Fauci-funded research “sparked COVID,” nor does it identify a specific NIH-funded virus that became SARS-CoV-2.
