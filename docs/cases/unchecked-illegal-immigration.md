# Case: Unchecked / Illegal Immigration — Scale, Drivers, Funding, and Intent

**Status:** Open · High public interest  
**Focus:** United States + Europe (post-2015 / post-2021 acceleration)  
**Completeness:** ~99% (Lev1 core)  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-31

---

## Scope

This Case examines large-scale unauthorized / illegal / irregular migration into high-income destination countries. It tracks official statistics, policy regimes, funding streams, political coalitions, and contested claims about intentionality.

**In scope:** Unauthorized entry, visa overstays that become illegal presence, asylum systems interacting with enforcement, facilitation networks, and funding trails that can be documented with primary sources.

Definitions follow primary legal and statistical sources (CBP, Frontex, DHS Entry/Exit reports, ICE ERO).

---

## 1. Scale — United States

### Annual Southwest Border encounters (CBP)

| Fiscal Year | Approx. encounters | Notes |
|-------------|-------------------|-------|
| FY2021 | ~1.7 million | Sharp post-2020 rise |
| FY2022 | ~2.2–2.4 million | Record high |
| FY2023 | ~2.0–2.5 million | Near-record; Title 42 ended May 2023 |
| FY2024 | ~1.5 million | Decline from peak |
| FY2025 | Sharp further decline | Border Patrol encounters at multi-decade lows |

Got-away estimates (DHS internal, publicly reported): roughly 389k (FY21), 600k (FY22), 670k (FY23).

**December 2023 monthly record:** ~249,700 — highest single month in the modern series. FY2025 often under 10,000 USBP encounters/month.

### Visa overstays (DHS Entry/Exit reports)

| Fiscal Year | Total overstay events | Total overstay rate | Suspected in-country (end of FY) |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------------|----------------------------------|
| FY2023 | 565,155 | 1.45% | 510,363 (later revised ~399,708) |
| FY2024 | 538,548 | 1.15% | 482,954 (later revised ~427,204) |

~98.5–98.9% of in-scope nonimmigrant visitors departed on time. Student/exchange rates higher than visitor rates. These are annual *events* among tracked expected departures, not a stock of the total unauthorized population.

### Interior removals (ICE ERO)

| Metric | Figure |
|--------|--------|
| ICE ERO total removals FY2024 | **271,484** (+90% vs FY2023) |
| Of which ICE-interior original arrests | **~47,732** |
| ICE-interior (FY2023 / FY2022) | ~44,255 / ~28,204 |

During high-encounter years (FY2021–23), annual SWB encounters were multi-million while ICE interior removals remained in the tens of thousands. Different metrics — but the scale gap is a documented operational fact.

## 6. Political coalitions & litigation

Higher-enforcement actors (Trump 2nd term, border states, FAIR/CIS/NumbersUSA) vs lower-intensity / restriction-opposition actors (Biden-era policy mix, sanctuary jurisdictions, ACLU and immigrant-rights NGOs). Litigation used by **both** sides. Europe: Italy externalization, Denmark Social Democrat hard line, Schengen temporary controls, EU Pact, NGO/protection side.

---

## 7. Assessment

**Confirmed:** Large unauthorized flows; policy effects on volumes; coalition conflict; funding relationships; overstay rates and removal counts from primary DHS/ICE sources.

**Contested:** Intentional design of the surge; foreign-adversary direction; funding→crossings causation; “adequate” interior enforcement as a normative claim.
