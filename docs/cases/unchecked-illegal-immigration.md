## Scope

Large-scale unauthorized / illegal / irregular migration into high-income destination countries. Official statistics, policy regimes, funding, political coalitions, contested intentionality claims.

## 1. Scale — United States

### Southwest Border encounters (CBP)

| FY | Approx. encounters |
|----|-------------------|
| 2021 | ~1.7M |
| 2022 | ~2.2–2.4M |
| 2023 | ~2.0–2.5M |
| 2024 | ~1.5M |
| 2025 | Historic lows (often <10k USBP/month) |

**Dec 2023 monthly record:** ~249,700. Got-aways FY21–23 roughly 389k / 600k / 670k.

### Citizenship composition (directional)

Pre-2021: Mexico + Northern Triangle majority. Surge years: major CHNV expansion + extra-continental nationalities. Exact rank-order tables: CBP Data Portal.

### Visa overstays (DHS Entry/Exit)

| FY | Events | Rate |
|----|--------|------|
| 2023 | 565,155 | 1.45% |
| 2024 | 538,548 | 1.15% |

~98.5–98.9% of in-scope visitors departed on time.
