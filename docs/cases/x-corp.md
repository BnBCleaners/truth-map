# Case: X (formerly Twitter)

**Status:** Core stages complete (~82%)  
**Date range:** 2022 acquisition–present (platform founded 2006)

---

## Summary

X is the social-media platform formerly known as Twitter, Inc. Elon Musk acquired it in a going-private transaction that closed on **27 October 2022** for approximately **$44 billion** ($54.20 per share cash). Financing combined equity, bank debt (~$13B), and a margin loan (~$12.5B).

Post-close the company rebranded to X, changed content-moderation posture (Community Notes expansion, reduced proactive enforcement of certain prior categories, reinstatement of previously banned accounts), and underwent major corporate restructuring:

- **xAI acquired X** (March 2025, all-stock; X valued at ~$33B equity / ~$45B including debt)
- **SpaceX acquired xAI** (February 2026, all-stock)

Financial performance after acquisition showed sharp advertising declines then partial recovery signals; debt service remained material.
