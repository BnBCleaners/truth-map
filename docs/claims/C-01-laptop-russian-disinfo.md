# Claim: Hunter Biden laptop reporting was Russian disinformation

**Claim ID:** C-01  
**Label:** Unsupported (later contradicted by forensic and legal record)  
**Status:** Active historical claim

---

## Plain-English version
In October 2020, 51 former intelligence officials signed a public statement saying the New York Post reporting on Hunter Biden’s laptop had “all the classic earmarks of Russian information operations.” The statement was widely used to justify suppression or heavy discounting of the story.

---

## What the later record shows
- The laptop was authenticated by the FBI (device matched to Hunter Biden’s iCloud ID).
- Independent forensic analyses (including media outlets that initially dismissed the story) confirmed thousands of emails.
- The laptop and its contents were introduced as evidence in Hunter Biden’s 2024 federal trials.
- No public evidence has established that the laptop material was a Russian fabrication.
