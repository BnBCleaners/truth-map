# Claim: “The Hunter Biden laptop was Russian disinformation”

**Label:** False

**Plain-English version of the claim:**  
In October 2020, 51 former intelligence officials said the New York Post’s Hunter Biden laptop story had “all the classic earmarks of a Russian information operation.” Many media outlets and the Biden campaign treated it as likely Russian disinformation.

**What the evidence shows:**  
- Evidence that supports it: None that held up. No intelligence community consensus ever confirmed it was a Russian operation. The letter itself only said it had “earmarks,” not that it was proven Russian.  
- Evidence that goes against it:  
  - The laptop was authenticated by the FBI, multiple forensic examinations, and later by court proceedings.  
  - Emails and data on the laptop matched known accounts and were used in subsequent investigations and reporting.  
  - No credible public evidence emerged that the laptop or its contents were fabricated by Russian intelligence.  
- Missing pieces: The original letter never claimed definitive proof — it used suggestive language that was then amplified.

**Why it is marked this way:**  
The core claim that it was Russian disinformation did not survive contact with the actual evidence. The laptop was real. Calling it Russian disinformation was a political narrative that collapsed.

**Who pushes this claim and who pushes back:**  
- Pushed by: The 51 former intelligence officials, Biden campaign, and many mainstream media outlets in late 2020.  
- Pushed back by: Forensic experts, later court records, House investigations, and subsequent reporting that treated the laptop as authentic.

**Sources:**  
- House Intelligence / Oversight interim reports on the 51-letter  
- FBI and forensic authentication records  
- Court use of laptop material  
- Original 19 Oct 2020 public statement
