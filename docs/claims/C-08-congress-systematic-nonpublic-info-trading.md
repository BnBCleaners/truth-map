# C-08 — Congress systematically trades using non-public information

**Claim ID:** C-08  
**Label:** Contested  
**Parent Case:** Congressional / Executive Stock Trading  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-31

---

**Plain-English claim:**  
Members of the House and Senate (or their spouses) routinely use information from hearings, briefings, or legislative work to make profitable stock trades — that this is a systemic pattern, not isolated incidents.

---

## Evidence that supports the claim (or the appearance that fuels it)

- Official Periodic Transaction Reports show that many members and spouses trade individual stocks, sometimes in high volume or high value (e.g., high trade-count and high disclosed-value names).  
- Academic and journalistic studies have claimed abnormal returns for some congressional portfolios (especially pre-STOCK Act literature).  
- Late STOCK Act filings are relatively common; formal public penalties are rare.  
- Multiple bills to ban individual stock ownership by members have been introduced, reflecting sustained public and political concern.

## Evidence that goes against or limits the claim

- No systemic pattern of criminal convictions or SEC enforcement actions under the STOCK Act for use of non-public information has been documented in the sources reviewed.  
- Some post-STOCK Act academic analyses (Belmont 2022; Chen & Sacerdote NBER 2026) find no statistically significant market-beating edge once proper controls are applied, or attribute apparent performance to broader market factors and public signals.  
- Volume of trading is not the same as proof of non-public information use.  
- Disclosure itself is required by law; the existence of visible trades does not establish systematic impropriety.

## Primary missing link

The same feet-check gap as C-07: documented cases that move from “well-timed trade” to “specific non-public information used in that trade” with primary evidence remain scarce. Aggregate studies that survive controls lean against a reliable systemic edge.

**Label remains Contested.** Appearance and volume are real; proof of systematic non-public-information use at scale is not established on the current record.
