# Claim: “NIH has not ever and does not now fund gain-of-function research at the Wuhan Institute of Virology”

**Claim ID:** C-03  
**Label:** Contested / Definition-dependent (often presented in a misleading way)  
**Status:** Active (core funding & risk claim)  
**Completeness:** 84%  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-05  
**Residual status:** Core evidence package (grant records, DEFUSE, debarment, Fauci diary primary) residual-clean at current public depth. Internal NIH P3CO / 2014-pause determination memos for EcoHealth–WIV experiments remain a standing residual (listed in FOIA tracker).

---

## 1. Plain-English version of the claim

Anthony Fauci (and NIH more broadly) stated under oath and in public that the NIH “has not ever and does not now fund gain-of-function research in the Wuhan Institute of Virology.”

The claim is used to deny that U.S. taxpayer money supported risky enhancement of bat coronaviruses at the WIV.

---

## 2. What the evidence shows

### Evidence that supports a *narrow* version of the claim
- NIH and NIAID applied a specific regulatory definition tied to the 2014–2017 U.S. funding pause and the later HHS P3CO framework.
- Under that narrow definition, the specific experiments funded under EcoHealth Alliance grant R01AI110964 were reviewed and determined *not* to meet the exact regulatory criteria.
- Published analyses conclude the bat SARSr-CoVs studied under the grant are genetically distant from SARS-CoV-2.

### Evidence that goes against the broad public / ordinary-language understanding
- **Documented experimental work:** Under R01AI110964, EcoHealth issued subawards to WIV. Work included construction of chimeric (recombinant) coronaviruses and testing in human cells and humanized (hACE2) mice. Some chimeras showed greater weight loss or pathogenicity than the parental backbone. Reporting of certain results was delayed.
- **Ordinary scientific meaning of “gain-of-function”:** Many virologists treat deliberate creation of chimeric viruses that acquire enhanced ability to infect human systems as gain-of-function, regardless of the narrow regulatory box.
- **Oversight findings:** Congressional reports concluded EcoHealth used taxpayer dollars to facilitate gain-of-function-style research at WIV. HHS later suspended and debarred EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak (2024–2025).
- **DEFUSE proposal (2018, unfunded):** EcoHealth + WIV + UNC proposed inserting human-specific proteolytic cleavage sites (including furin-like sites) into bat SARSr-CoVs. DARPA rejected it.
- **Fauci 2021 diary (primary):** Contemporaneous private notes show defensiveness about GOF and lab-origin questions. He characterized many inquiries as “conspiracy theory like letters” and a “waste of our time,” while asserting the specific EcoHealth-grant viruses could not have become SARS-CoV-2.

---

## 3. Why the claim is labeled Contested / Definition-dependent (often misleading)

The statement is technically defensible under NIH’s narrow post-2014 regulatory definition.  
It is misleading in ordinary public discourse because the funded work involved deliberate genetic manipulation of SARS-related bat coronaviruses to test and, in some cases, enhance their ability to infect human systems, and the public denial created the impression that no risky enhancement work had been supported with U.S. funds at WIV.
