# C-05 — “Gavin Newsom is Nancy Pelosi’s nephew”

**Claim ID:** C-05  
**Label:** False  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-28

---

**Plain-English claim:**  
Many social media posts and repeated online statements claim that California Governor Gavin Newsom is the nephew of Nancy Pelosi.

---

## What the evidence shows

- **Evidence that supports the stronger claim:** None. There is no blood relationship.  
- **Evidence that goes against it:**  
  - Gavin Newsom’s aunt, Barbara (Belinda) Newsom, was married to Ron Pelosi (brother of Paul Pelosi, Nancy’s husband).  
  - That marriage ended in divorce in 1977. Barbara Newsom died in 2008.  
  - This made Nancy Pelosi an aunt-by-marriage to Gavin Newsom only during his childhood, through a link that dissolved nearly 50 years ago.  
  - Multiple independent fact-checks (FactCheck.org, ABC7, CalMatters, The Dispatch, and others) have documented the same genealogy and labeled the “nephew” claim false.

**Why it is marked False:**  
Calling him her “nephew” is a clear exaggeration. The actual connection is distant, by marriage only, and ended decades ago. Repeating the stronger claim turns a weak family-network detail into a false statement of close blood relation.
