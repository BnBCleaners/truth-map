# C-11: Operation Mockingbird Never Ended / Continues Today

**Label:** Contested  
**Related Case:** Project Mockingbird  
**Related:** Media Influence portfolio · U10 Twitter Files package

## Claim
“Operation Mockingbird” (or its functional equivalent) never ended after the Church Committee and 1976–77 CIA policy changes. The same continuous program, or a direct evolved version, continues to control or direct editorial lines in major U.S. media — including via modern platform moderation and fact-checker networks.

## Evidence supporting elements
- Cold War relationships were extensive; residual informal ties are plausible.
- Pattern evidence of coordinated narrative alignment on intelligence-sensitive stories that later proved incomplete (lab-leak early framing, 2020 laptop treatment, etc.).
- Twitter Files documented government–platform contacts that shaped story visibility — functional parallel even if organizationally different.

## Evidence against the strongest form
- No primary-document equivalent of a continuous formal “Mockingbird” org chart into the present.
- Organizational form of platform trust-and-safety + government portals differs from recruited-journalist networks.
- Ordinary ideological clustering and professional norms also produce alignment without a directed program.

## Why Contested
Historical relationships and modern government–platform contacts are documented. Identity of a single continuous control program is not established. Both the continuity claim and the counter-analyses are retained so the sifting stays visible.
