# NIAID / NIH Intramural High-Containment Cluster

**Type:** Research laboratories (high-containment)  
**Operator:** NIAID / NIH  
**Locations:** IRF-Frederick (Fort Detrick, MD); Rocky Mountain Laboratories (Hamilton, MT); Bethesda campus

---

## Key documented facts
- **IRF-Frederick:** NIAID high-containment facility for pathogenesis and medical countermeasure studies.
- **Rocky Mountain Laboratories (RML):** Long-standing NIAID laboratory with high-containment capability.
- **Bethesda campus:** Additional NIAID/NIH containment capacity.
- Tracked as a cluster so the U.S. intramural landscape is visible alongside WIV and the extramural NBLs.
