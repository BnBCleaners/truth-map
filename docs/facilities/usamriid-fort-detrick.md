# USAMRIID / Fort Detrick

**Type:** Military laboratory (BSL-4)  
**Operator:** U.S. Army Medical Research Institute of Infectious Diseases  
**Location:** Fort Detrick, Frederick, Maryland, USA

---

## Key documented facts
- Principal U.S. Army BSL-4 laboratory for defensive biodefense research.
- Historical role in Amerithrax investigation.
- 2019 CDC temporary suspension of select-agent research after inspection findings; later resumed.
- Tracked as a comparative high-containment node alongside WIV.
