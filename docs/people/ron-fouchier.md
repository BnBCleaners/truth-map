# Ron A.M. Fouchier

**Role:** Professor and Deputy Head, Department of Viroscience, Erasmus Medical Center (Rotterdam)  
**Primary relevance:** 2011–2012 H5N1 airborne-transmission GOF experiments; participant on the 1 February 2020 origins call who argued natural origin

---

## What matters here

### Documented Gain-of-Function history
- 2011–2012 H5N1 ferret-transmission experiments. Fouchier’s team produced a virus capable of airborne mammalian transmission. He publicly described it as “probably one of the most dangerous viruses you can make.”
- Triggered international biosecurity crisis, NSABB review, publication delays, and contributed to the later U.S. funding pause on certain GOF research of concern.

### 1 February 2020 origins call (Fauci diary)
- Per Fauci’s contemporaneous diary: Fouchier argued he was “sure that this could occur naturally” and that the group “should not waste our time and divert effort to pursue” a laboratory scenario. Christian Drosten aligned with him; “the rest” felt deliberate insertion remained possible.
