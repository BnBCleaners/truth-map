# Peter Daszak

**Role:** President of EcoHealth Alliance  
**Primary relevance:** PI on NIH bat-coronavirus grant with WIV subawards; organizer of the February 2020 *Lancet* letter; long-time collaborator with Shi Zhengli / WIV

---

## What matters here

### Funding & WIV collaboration
- Principal Investigator on NIH/NIAID grant R01AI110964. EcoHealth issued subawards to WIV for bat SARS-related coronavirus work, including chimeric constructs tested in human cells and humanized mice.
- Long-standing research collaboration with Shi Zhengli and WIV.
- 2018 DEFUSE proposal (EcoHealth lead, with WIV and UNC): proposed inserting human-specific proteolytic cleavage sites into bat SARSr-CoVs. DARPA rejected the proposal.

### February 2020 *Lancet* letter
- Organized and drafted the letter that condemned “conspiracy theories suggesting that COVID-19 does not have a natural origin.” Initial declaration of no competing interests; later *Lancet* addendum after public criticism of the undisclosed WIV relationship.

### Oversight outcome
- HHS suspended and then debarred EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak (2024–2025).
