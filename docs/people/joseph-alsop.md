# Joseph Alsop

**Who:** American syndicated newspaper columnist, one of the most influential political writers of the mid-20th century.  
**Role:** Co-author (with brother Stewart) of the “Matter of Fact” column; later independent columnist. Peak influence 1940s–1960s.

**Why this card exists:** Carl Bernstein’s 1977 investigation documented that Joseph Alsop carried out assignments for the CIA, including travel to the Philippines (1953) and Laos (1952). Clear example of witting cooperation between a high-profile journalist and the Agency.

**Label on CIA relationship:** **True** (documented cooperative assignments). Degree of formal control Contested.

**Sources:** Bernstein, “The CIA and the Media,” *Rolling Stone*, 20 Oct 1977.
