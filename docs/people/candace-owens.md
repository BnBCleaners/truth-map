## Biographical snapshot

Born 1989. Early career in marketing and short-lived anti-bullying concept. Shifted to conservative commentary ~2017 (“Red Pill Black”), Turning Point USA roles, BLEXIT, PragerU, then joined The Daily Wire in March 2021 to host *Candace*. Married to George Farmer; four children. Independent after March 2024 Daily Wire exit.
