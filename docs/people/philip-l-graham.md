# Philip L. Graham

**Who:** Publisher of *The Washington Post* (and later *Newsweek*). Husband of Katharine Graham. Died 1963.

**Why this card exists:** Bernstein and Deborah Davis both place Philip Graham in a cooperative relationship with the CIA via Frank Wisner’s OPC channel. Central to the secondary “Operation Mockingbird” narrative about publisher recruitment.

**Label:** Cooperative relationship via Wisner channel supported by Bernstein + Davis; organizational “ran the domestic industry” claim Contested.
