# Jeremy Farrar (Sir Jeremy James Farrar)

**Role:** Former Director of the Wellcome Trust (2013–2023); Chief Scientist, World Health Organization (from 2023)  
**Primary relevance:** Central convenor of the earliest high-level scientific discussions on the origin of SARS-CoV-2

---

## What matters here

### Early 2020 origins calls (Fauci diary)
- **31 January 2020:** Called Fauci, sounding very concerned, and conferenced in Kristian Andersen (Holmes also involved). The scientists on the phone felt the furin cleavage site mutations “could not have occurred naturally” and raised deliberate insertion + lab release as possibilities.
- **1 February 2020:** Structured the larger multi-scientist conference call. Majority felt deliberate insertion possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s GOF work; Fouchier and Drosten argued natural origin. Fauci wrote “we could not let this go.”

### Proximal Origin and Lancet letter
- Facilitated aspects of the Proximal Origin drafting process.
- Signatory of the February 2020 *Lancet* letter that framed non-natural origin ideas as conspiracy theories.
