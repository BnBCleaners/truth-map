# Topic: Unchecked / Illegal Immigration

**Hub for multi-angle analysis of unauthorized migration into the U.S. and Europe.**

Complex subjects are not single Claims. This Topic holds the scored Angles so the sifting stays visible.

---

## Quick synopsis

Large unauthorized flows into the United States and Europe are documented in official border statistics (CBP, Frontex). Volumes peaked in 2023 on both sides of the Atlantic, then declined — sharply in the U.S. by FY2025. Policy choices (Title 42 end, parole programs, release practices, post-release funding) had measurable effects. Political coalitions consistently pushed opposite directions of enforcement intensity. Claims that the surge was *intentionally designed* to increase illegal entries remain **Contested**.
