## Core documented facts (True)

- Bill Clinton founded the Clinton Foundation after leaving office (2001). Primary legal name today: Bill, Hillary & Chelsea Clinton Foundation (501(c)(3)).  
- Clinton Global Initiative (CGI) remains active.  
- During Hillary Clinton’s tenure as Secretary of State (2009–2013), the Foundation accepted millions from foreign governments. A 2008 ethics MOU governed prior vs. new donors; at least one compliance failure (Algeria) is documented.  
- Bill Clinton took multiple documented flights on Jeffrey Epstein’s aircraft in 2002–2003 (~26 legs / 4 trips) for foundation-related travel.  
- Island visits and knowledge of Epstein’s crimes: no primary evidence on the current record for either Clinton.  
- Uranium One / Rosatom acquisition, CFIUS multi-agency review, key industry donations, and the $500k Bill Clinton Moscow speech (June 2010) are documented. Strong popular “sold 20% of U.S. uranium for $145M” framing is Contested / False in that form.
