# Topic: COVID-19 Origins

**Type:** Multi-angle Topic  
**Status:** Active  
**Last structural update:** July 2026

This is not a single claim. It is the public subject that contains multiple distinct factual questions about how SARS-CoV-2 emerged and how that question was handled. Each question is scored separately as an Angle.

---

## Short synopsis

In early 2020 a strong public narrative formed that treating a laboratory origin as a serious possibility was itself a conspiracy theory. At the same time, private scientific discussion (now documented in primary sources including the Fauci diary) took laboratory scenarios seriously. Parallel disputes continue over whether U.S.-funded work at the Wuhan Institute of Virology constituted gain-of-function research under ordinary scientific meaning versus narrow regulatory definitions.

The public argument collapsed these into one or two slogans. The actual work requires separating the angles.
