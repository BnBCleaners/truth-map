# Bilderberg

<span class="badge badge-contested">Elite Forum</span>

**Primary Type:** Private annual conference  
**Secondary tags:** Transatlantic elite · Media executives often invited

## Why this card exists
Recurring private meeting of political, business, academic, and media figures. Tracked because media executives and editors appear on participant lists — relevant to structural media-influence questions, not as proof of a command structure.

## Documented facts
- First meeting 1954. Invitation-only; discussions off-record (Chatham House-style).
- Participant lists are partially public after the fact for many years.
- Includes heads of government, EU officials, major corporate leaders, and senior journalists/editors in various years.

## Contested framings
“Secret world government” and similar control claims are Contested. Attendance is a documented fact; collective decision-making authority is not established by attendance lists alone.
