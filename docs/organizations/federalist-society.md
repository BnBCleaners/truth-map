# Federalist Society

<span class="badge badge-contested">Legal Network</span>

**What it is:** Network of conservative and libertarian lawyers, law students, and judges (founded 1982). Dominant talent pipeline for Republican judicial appointments.

**Documented facts:** Six of nine current Supreme Court justices have clear Federalist Society ties or affiliations. Leonard Leo was a central figure in generating and vetting nominee lists used by Trump administrations. Significant donor flows through related networks (partially opaque / dark-money components documented by investigative reporting).

**Contested framings:** Ordinary network influence vs. improper capture claims remain Contested on specifics; the pipeline role itself is well documented.

**Cross-links:** US government / judiciary tracking · elite network set
