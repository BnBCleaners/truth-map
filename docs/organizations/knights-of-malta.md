# Sovereign Military Order of Malta (Knights of Malta)

<span class="badge badge-contested">Sovereign Order</span>

**What it is:** Catholic lay religious order with sovereign-entity status under international law. Humanitarian and diplomatic activities; historically military/hospitaller roots.

**Documented facts:** Maintains diplomatic relations with numerous states. Membership has included prominent political and intelligence-adjacent figures in various countries over time.

**Contested framings:** Control / intelligence-cutout claims beyond documented diplomatic and humanitarian roles are Contested.
