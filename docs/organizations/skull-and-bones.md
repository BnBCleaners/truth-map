# Skull and Bones (Yale)

<span class="badge badge-contested">Secret Society</span>

**What it is:** Oldest senior secret society at Yale (founded 1832). Selects 15 seniors per year. Alumni network is the long-term structure (Russell Trust Association).

**Documented facts:** Produces a dense alumni network that has repeatedly appeared at high levels of U.S. government, intelligence, finance, and media. Three U.S. Presidents were members (Taft, GHW Bush, GW Bush). GHW Bush later served as CIA Director. John Kerry (Bonesman) ran against GW Bush (Bonesman) in 2004.

**Contested framings:** Control / conspiracy claims beyond elite networking are Contested. Membership is not treated as proof of directed coordination.
