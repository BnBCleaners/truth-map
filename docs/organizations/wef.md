# World Economic Forum (WEF / Davos)

<span class="badge badge-contested">Elite Forum</span>

**Primary Type:** International public-private convening organization  
**Secondary tags:** Davos · Stakeholder capitalism · Media amplification

## Why this card exists
High-visibility annual meeting and year-round programs that bring together political, corporate, and media leadership. Tracked for structural elite-network and narrative-amplification questions.

## Documented facts
- Founded by Klaus Schwab (1971). Annual meeting in Davos, Switzerland.
- Publishes agendas around public-private cooperation, climate, technology, and governance themes.
- Young Global Leaders and other programs have included many later prominent politicians and executives.

## Contested framings
Claims of WEF “control” over national governments or media are Contested. Agenda-setting influence via elite networking is a weaker, more plausible claim that still requires case-by-case evidence.
