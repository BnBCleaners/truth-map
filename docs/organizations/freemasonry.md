# Freemasonry

<span class="badge badge-contested">Fraternal Order</span>

**What it is:** Global fraternal order with roots in early modern speculative masonry. Lodges, degrees, and appendant bodies (including Scottish Rite). Publicly presents itself as a moral and charitable brotherhood.

**Documented facts:** Large historical membership among political, military, and civic elites in many countries. Degree structures and some ritual texts are partially public. No single central command runs all lodges worldwide.

**Contested framings:** Claims of unified global political control are Contested. Local influence networks have existed; a single directed world program is not established by membership alone.
