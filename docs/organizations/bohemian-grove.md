# Bohemian Grove / Bohemian Club

<span class="badge badge-contested">Private Club</span>

**What it is:** Private, invitation-only, all-male social club (founded 1872, San Francisco). Annual summer encampment at Bohemian Grove (~2,700 acres, Monte Rio, California) with informal socializing, Lakeside Talks, and rituals including the “Cremation of Care.”

**Documented facts:** Guest and member lists over decades have included U.S. presidents, Cabinet officials, corporate CEOs, and media figures. It is a classic elite social institution, not a formal policy body.

**Contested framings:** Claims of policy-making cabals or occult control are Contested. Attendance is documented; collective governing authority is not established by attendance alone.
