## Foreign-government donors during State tenure (2009–2013)

**Governing framework (True):**  
A 2008 Memorandum of Understanding / ethics arrangement with the Obama administration distinguished prior donors (generally permitted to continue) from new foreign-government donors (required prior State Department ethics approval). Transparency commitments were part of the agreement.

**Documented fact (True):**  
The Foundation accepted **millions of dollars from seven foreign governments** while Hillary Clinton served as Secretary of State. At least one contribution (commonly identified with Algeria) was accepted without the new-donor ethics approval required by the MOU — a disclosed compliance failure.
