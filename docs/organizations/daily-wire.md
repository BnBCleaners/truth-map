## Funding & institutional model

**Seed capital (True)**  
- 2015: $4.7 million seed investment from Farris Wilks (Texas petroleum / fracking billionaire; brother Dan Wilks also associated with the family’s conservative media investments).  
- Farris Wilks has been described in multiple reports as remaining an owner or significant stakeholder. Exact current equity percentages are not fully public.

**Revenue mix (publicly described)**  
- Primary: Paid subscriptions (DailyWire+).  
- Secondary: Digital advertising.  
- Tertiary: E-commerce / branded products (Jeremy’s Razors etc.); Axios reported >$22 million commerce revenue in 2023.  
- Additional: Content licensing, film/TV, books.  
- Company has publicly claimed periods of strong revenue growth (exceeding $100M annually by early 2020s) and valuations “north of $1 billion” in recent private raises.

**Transparency:** Partial on precise current cap table; seed amount and Wilks role are well-documented.
