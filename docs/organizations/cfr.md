# Council on Foreign Relations (CFR)

<span class="badge badge-contested">Elite Forum</span>

**Primary Type:** Elite policy forum / membership organization  
**Secondary tags:** Foreign policy · Media overlap · Government pipeline

## Why this card exists
Long historical overlap between CFR membership and leadership of major U.S. media, government, and finance. Tracked for cross-reference to media-influence and elite-network Angles — not as proof of control.

## Documented facts
- Founded 1921. Non-partisan membership organization focused on U.S. foreign policy.
- Publishes *Foreign Affairs*. Hosts meetings and task forces.
- Membership historically includes senior journalists, editors, publishers, Cabinet officials, and business leaders.
- Attendance and membership are public facts for many individuals; they are not treated in this KB as evidence of directed editorial control.

## Contested framings (recorded, not adopted)
Claims that CFR “runs” U.S. foreign policy or media are widespread in contested literature. Those claims require primary evidence of direction beyond membership overlap and are labeled **Contested** here.
