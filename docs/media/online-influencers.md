# Online Influencers & Digital Narrative Tracking

**Status:** Phase 1 + Option A complete (public mirror)  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-01 / 2026-08-02

High-reach online influencers whose public stances, documented stance *changes*, funding/monetization, and platform relationships are material to topics already in the KB. Modern parallel to traditional Media Influence. Same evidence discipline, same five-term labels.

---

## Inclusion rules (locked)

- Clear documented relevance to existing KB topics **or** high narrative impact on those topics **or** a documented funding / stance-shift signal that is itself material.  
- No popularity rankings. No membership dumps.  
- Association (shared stage, mutual follows, same conference) is association only.  
- “Paid to change” or “network pressure caused the shift” starts as **Contested** unless strong primary evidence of the exchange or directive exists.  
- Every causal claim about *why* a stance changed receives its own explicit label.

**Standardized stance topics (Phase 1):** COVID origins / lab-leak · Vaccine policy & mandates · 2020/2024 election integrity · Israel/Gaza & antisemitism framing · Ukraine/Russia · China/CCP · Free speech & platform censorship · U.S. intelligence community trust · Trump trajectory (when relevant).
