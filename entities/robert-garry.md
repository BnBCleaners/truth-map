## What matters here

### 1 February 2020 origins call (Fauci diary)
- Participant on the multi-scientist call.
- Per the diary, Fouchier and Drosten argued natural origin; “the rest” felt deliberate insertion remained possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s GOF work. Garry is part of the core Proximal Origin author group that continued the analysis after the call.

### Proximal Origin paper
- Co-author of the March 2020 *Nature Medicine* paper.
- Early private communications among the authors show the group initially took the furin cleavage site and related features seriously as potential indicators of laboratory involvement before the published paper ruled laboratory construction out.
