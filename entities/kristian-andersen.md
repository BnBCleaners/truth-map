## What matters here

### Early 2020 origins calls (Fauci diary + FOIA)
- **31 January 2020:** Sent email to Fauci flagging unusual genomic features (especially the furin cleavage site). Later that day conferenced by Jeremy Farrar into a call with Fauci; Eddie Holmes also involved. Fauci’s diary records that the scientists on the phone felt the mutations “could not have occurred naturally” and raised deliberate insertion + lab release as possibilities.
- **1 February 2020:** Participated in the larger multi-scientist call. Majority felt deliberate insertion possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s GOF work; Fouchier and Drosten argued natural origin.

### Proximal Origin paper
- Lead/corresponding author of the drafting process that produced the March 2020 paper.
- Early drafts and private communications (FOIA + later Slack) show higher laboratory-origin probability estimates among the core authors before the published paper stated the virus was “not a laboratory construct or a purposefully manipulated virus.”
