## What matters here

### Early 2020 origins calls (Fauci diary)
- **31 January 2020:** Involved in the Farrar–Andersen call with Fauci. Diary records that the scientists on the phone felt the mutations around the furin cleavage site “could not have occurred naturally” and raised deliberate insertion + lab release as possibilities.
- **1 February 2020:** Participant on the larger multi-scientist call. Majority (“the rest”) felt deliberate insertion remained possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s GOF work; Fouchier and Drosten argued natural origin.

### Proximal Origin paper
- Co-author of the March 2020 *Nature Medicine* paper that concluded the virus was “not a laboratory construct or a purposefully manipulated virus.”
- Early private communications among the authors show non-trivial laboratory-origin probability estimates before the published conclusion.
