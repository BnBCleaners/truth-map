# Peter Daszak

**Card Type:** Focused  
**Who:** President of EcoHealth Alliance; zoologist / disease ecologist  
**Primary Role(s):** PI on NIH bat-coronavirus grant with WIV subawards; organizer of the February 2020 *Lancet* letter; long-time collaborator with Shi Zhengli / WIV  
**Completeness:** High on documented roles

---

## What matters here

### Funding & WIV collaboration
- Principal Investigator on NIH/NIAID grant R01AI110964 (“Understanding the Risk of Bat Coronavirus Emergence”). EcoHealth issued subawards to the Wuhan Institute of Virology for bat SARS-related coronavirus work, including chimeric constructs tested in human cells and humanized mice.
- Long-standing research collaboration with Shi Zhengli and WIV (joint papers, field sampling, experimental work).
- 2018 DEFUSE proposal (EcoHealth lead, with WIV and UNC): proposed inserting human-specific proteolytic cleavage sites (including furin-like sites) into bat SARSr-CoVs. DARPA rejected the proposal.

### February 2020 *Lancet* letter
- Organized and drafted the letter that condemned “conspiracy theories suggesting that COVID-19 does not have a natural origin.” Initial declaration of no competing interests; later *Lancet* addendum after public criticism of the undisclosed WIV relationship.

### Oversight outcome
- HHS suspended and then debarred EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak (2024–2025) citing material non-compliance, failure to report, and related issues.
