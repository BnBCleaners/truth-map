## What matters here

### Documented Gain-of-Function history
- 2011–2012 H5N1 ferret-transmission experiments (parallel to Yoshihiro Kawaoka’s work). Fouchier’s team produced a virus capable of airborne mammalian transmission. He publicly described it as “probably one of the most dangerous viruses you can make.”
- Triggered international biosecurity crisis, NSABB review, publication delays, and contributed to the later U.S. funding pause on certain GOF research of concern.
- Long-standing public defender of the scientific value and manageability of carefully conducted GOF experiments.

### 1 February 2020 origins call (Fauci diary)
- Participant on the multi-scientist call.
- Per Fauci’s contemporaneous diary: Fouchier argued he was “sure that this could occur naturally” and that the group “should not waste our time and divert effort to pursue” a laboratory scenario. Christian Drosten aligned with him; “the rest” felt deliberate insertion remained possible and referenced Shi Zhengli’s GOF work.
