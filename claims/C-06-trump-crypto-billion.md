# Claim: Donald Trump and associated entities realized more than $1 billion in personal financial benefit from World Liberty Financial and related tokens

**Claim ID:** C-06  
**Label:** Supported (leaning Confirmed on the $1B threshold for 2025 crypto-related income)  
**Status:** Active  
**Source Issue:** [#3](https://github.com/BnBCleaners/truth-map/issues/3) (usability test contribution)

---

## 1. Plain-English version of the claim

Donald Trump and associated entities realized more than $1 billion in personal financial benefit from World Liberty Financial (WLF) and related crypto tokens.

---

## 2. What the evidence shows

### Supporting
- **OGE annual financial disclosure (2025 income, released ~1 Jul 2026):** Trump reported more than $1.4 billion from family crypto ventures. More than $550 million specifically from World Liberty Financial token sales (vs. over $57 million in the prior-year disclosure). Primary source: U.S. Office of Government Ethics filing.
- **World Liberty Financial company materials:** Trump-affiliated entity entitled to 75% of net proceeds from WLFI token sales; equity stake in the parent reported in the 38–60% range depending on the disclosure version.
- **SEC Form D filings:** Document exempt offerings / token sales by World Liberty Financial, Inc.
- Secondary reporting (TIME, AP, PBS, ABC) consistently reflects the same OGE numbers.

### Boundaries / not established by this Claim
- Whether any specific transaction constituted illegal insider trading is a separate legal question and is **not** scored by C-06.
- Paper wealth in unsold tokens vs. realized cash proceeds should be distinguished when citing exact figures; the OGE income figures are the primary anchor for the “realized benefit” language.

### Contested framing to avoid
- Collapsing “WLF alone” and “all crypto ventures” without noting the disclosure breakdown.
- Treating media headlines as primary when the OGE filing is available.

---

## 3. Why the label is Supported (leaning Confirmed)

The $1 billion threshold for 2025 crypto-related income is directly supported by the official OGE financial disclosure. WLF is a major component (> $550M). Combined crypto ventures in the same filing clear $1B. Further primary-document page cites can move this fully to Confirmed.
