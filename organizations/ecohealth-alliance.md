# EcoHealth Alliance

**Type:** Non-profit / research organization  
**Primary relevance:** U.S. recipient of NIH/NIAID funding that subawarded bat coronavirus work to the Wuhan Institute of Virology; later suspended and debarred by HHS

---

## Key facts
- Received NIH grant R01AI110964 and related funding for bat coronavirus emergence research.
- Issued subawards to WIV for sampling, sequencing, and experimental work (including chimeric viruses tested in human-relevant systems).
- Peter Daszak served as President and PI on the key grants.
- Collaborated with WIV and University of North Carolina researchers on proposals including the 2018 DEFUSE proposal to DARPA (rejected).
- 2024–2025: HHS suspended then debarred EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak for multiple years, citing compliance failures and facilitation of the research under scrutiny.
