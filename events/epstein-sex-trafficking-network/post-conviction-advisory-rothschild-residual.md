## Summary (plain English)

After his 2008 Florida conviction, Jeffrey Epstein provided paid strategic and personal advisory services to Ariane de Rothschild and the Edmond de Rothschild Group. The relationship is documented in contemporaneous emails and formal agreements between 2013 and early 2019. The largest documented fee ($25 million) was contractually linked to the resolution of the bank’s U.S. regulatory exposure under the DOJ Swiss Bank Program. A second agreement/invoice for additional sums appears in the same period. The residual records the advisory relationship, fee structure, and network introductions on primary evidence only. No intelligence-service claims are carried.

**2019**
