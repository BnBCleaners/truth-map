# CASE: Fauci Senate Testimony on Gain-of-Function at WIV (May 2021)

**Case ID:** EVENT-2021-FAUCI-SENATE-GOF  
**Status:** Closed (testimony on record); Contested (on accuracy and framing)  
**Date Range:** Primary testimony 11 May 2021  
**Completeness:** 82%

---

## 1. Summary
On 11 May 2021, Dr. Anthony Fauci stated under questioning by Sen. Rand Paul that “the NIH has not ever and does not now fund gain-of-function research in the Wuhan Institute of Virology.” The statement sits at the center of Claim C-03. Subsequent congressional investigations, grant documents, and the 2024–2025 HHS debarment of EcoHealth Alliance and Peter Daszak have kept the accuracy and framing under scrutiny.

---

## 2. Key facts
- Exact quote is on the public record from the 11 May 2021 Senate hearing.
- NIH-funded EcoHealth grant R01AI110964 supported work at WIV that included construction and testing of chimeric SARS-related bat coronaviruses in human cells and humanized mice.
- NIH applied a narrow regulatory definition tied to the 2014–2017 funding pause and later P3CO framework; under that definition NIH maintained the work did not qualify.
- Many scientists and later congressional reports treated the same work as gain-of-function under ordinary scientific usage.
- **Fauci 2021 diary (primary):** Contemporaneous private notes show defensiveness about GOF and lab-origin questions. He characterized many inquiries as “conspiracy theory like letters” and a “waste of our time,” while asserting the specific EcoHealth-grant viruses could not have become SARS-CoV-2.
